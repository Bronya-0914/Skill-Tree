### Bilingual conventions

- **Paired files**: `.md` (Traditional Chinese) and `.en.md` (English), eight pairs.
- **Single-file bilingual**: `## 繁體中文` plus `## English` sections, or a bilingual table.
- Both kinds carry the live demo and repository URLs and use relative links only.

### Positioning consistency

Every document shares one premise: **the site ships with no pre-existing course**. Courses come from user-supplied material, are generated by an AI, and are rendered by the site. The design motive is visible progress and a sense of achievement on every completion. If the positioning ever changes, update the whole set, not just the main README.
